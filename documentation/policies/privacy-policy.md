## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

### Technical Safeguards

#### Encryption
- **In Transit:** TLS 1.3 for all client-server communication
- **At Rest:** AES-256 encryption for all stored data

#### Authentication
- Secure password hashing (never stored in plain text)
- **Web:** HTTP-only session and refresh cookies for sign-in (tokens are not readable by page JavaScript)
- **Client storage:** We do not store auth tokens in `localStorage`; non-sensitive UI state may use `sessionStorage` where needed
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) available when enabled

See [Security policy](./security.md) for deployment, CORS, and cookie details.

#### PII Protection in Logs
- **Automated Masking:** All logs automatically scrubbed for sensitive data
- **Patterns Detected:** Emails, phone numbers, SSN, credit cards, JWT tokens, API keys, passwords
- **Implementation:**
  - Frontend: `Client/packages/logger/pii.ts`
  - Backend: `Server/logger/pii.py`

#### Security Headers
- HSTS (enforce HTTPS)
- X-Frame-Options (prevent clickjacking)
- Content Security Policy (prevent XSS)
- X-Content-Type-Options (prevent MIME sniffing)

#### Input Validation
- Server-side validation for all user input
- Parameterized SQL queries (prevent injection attacks)
- File upload validation (MIME type, size, malware scanning)

### Organizational Safeguards

- Access controls (least privilege principle)
- Employee security training
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Incident response procedures
- Vendor security assessments

**No Security is Perfect:** While we use industry-standard protections, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and enable MFA.

## Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

### Retention Summary

- **Active Accounts:** Data retained while account is active
- **Inactive Accounts:** Deleted after 3 years of inactivity (with prior warning)
- **Deleted Accounts:** Permanently deleted within 90 days (except legal obligations)
- **Transaction Records:** Retained for 7 years (legal requirement for real estate transactions)
- **Application and error logs:** Retained for 90 days (PII-masked)
- **Security audit logs:** Retained for 1 year (PII-masked)
- **Backups:** Overwritten within 90 days of deletion request

## Cookies and Tracking

### What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us provide and improve our services.

### Cookies We Use

#### Essential Cookies (Required)
- **Purpose:** Authentication, security, session management
- **Cannot Be Disabled:** Required for platform functionality

#### Performance and diagnostics
- We may use first-party or strictly necessary technical data (including limited analytics or error
  reporting tied to your session) to operate and improve the Services. We do not use a separate
  in-product “cookie preferences” center today; you can use browser or device settings to manage
  storage where your platform allows it. Disabling essential storage may break sign-in.

#### No Advertising Cookies
- We do NOT use cookies for third-party advertising or cross-site tracking
- We do NOT share cookies with ad networks

### Cookie preferences

**Manage cookies and storage**
- **Web:** Use your browser’s settings to block or delete cookies and site data.
- **Mobile apps:** Use OS settings for the app where available.

**Note:** Disabling essential cookies or storage may prevent you from staying signed in.

### Global Privacy Control (GPC) and Do Not Track (DNT)

We read the `Sec-GPC` request header on our servers. When it indicates an opt-out (`Sec-GPC: 1`), we
treat that as a request to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information, consistent with
CCPA/CPRA (we do not “sell” personal information as that term is commonly defined; if our practices
change, this policy will be updated). We do not respond to browser DNT headers (there is no
ubiquitous standard for implementing them).
