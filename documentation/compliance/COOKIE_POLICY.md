# Cookie and storage policy (SilverKey)

**Last updated:** April 22, 2026

## Summary

SilverKey’s web and mobile applications rely primarily on **essential** storage mechanisms (for
example HTTP-only session cookies on the web, and platform storage needed to keep you signed in) to
deliver core functionality. We do not use a dedicated third-party advertising or cross-site
tracking stack in the product as described in our [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## What we use

| Category        | Purpose                                      | How to control                          |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|
| Essential auth | Sign-in, session security, anti-abuse        | Required to use the Services            |
| Preferences    | UI state and product settings where needed   | In-app where applicable; otherwise OS |

## Global Privacy Control (GPC)

Our API layer reads the `Sec-GPC` header. See [Privacy Policy — GPC](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for how we
treat opt-out signals.

## Changes

We may update this page when our practices or integrations change. The Privacy Policy governs
personal data; this document is a focused summary for cookies and similar storage.
