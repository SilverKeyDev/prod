## Overview

SilverKey ("we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy rights of California consumers under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2023 (CPRA)

**Applicability:** This notice applies to California residents whose personal information is subject to CCPA/CPRA.

---

## Definitions

- **Consumer:** A California resident as defined by California law
- **Personal Information:** Information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is capable of being associated with a particular consumer or household
- **Sale:** Sharing personal information for monetary or other valuable consideration
- **Sharing:** Disclosing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising
- **Service Provider:** A third party that processes personal information on our behalf for business purposes
- **Third Party:** An entity other than SilverKey or our service providers

## California Consumer Rights

Under CCPA/CPRA, California consumers have the following rights:

### 1. Right to Know (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100, § 1798.110, § 1798.115)

**You have the right to request:**
- Categories of personal information we have collected about you
- Specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you
- Categories of sources from which we collected personal information
- Business or commercial purposes for collecting personal information
- Categories of third parties with whom we share personal information
- Categories of personal information sold or shared (if any)
- Categories of personal information disclosed for business purposes

**Request Frequency:** Up to 2 requests per 12-month period
**Response Time:** Within 45 days (extendable to 90 days with notice)

### 2. Right to Delete (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.105)

**You have the right to request deletion of personal information we collected from you**, subject to certain exceptions (e.g., legal obligations, fraud prevention).

**Scope:** We will delete or de-identify your personal information from our records and direct service providers to do the same.

**Exceptions:** We may retain data to:
- Complete transactions or provide services
- Detect security incidents or fraud
- Comply with legal obligations (e.g., transaction records)
- Exercise free speech or other rights
- Engage in research (if deletion would impair research)
- Internal uses reasonably aligned with consumer expectations

**Response Time:** Within 45 days (extendable to 90 days with notice)

### 3. Right to Correct (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.106)

**You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal information.**

**How:** Submit correction request via privacy@silverkey.com or in-app settings
**Response Time:** Within 45 days (extendable to 90 days with notice)

### 4. Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.120)

**You have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information.**

**Current Status:** SilverKey does **NOT** sell or share personal information as defined by CCPA/CPRA. We do not:
- Sell personal information for monetary compensation
- Share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising

**How to Opt Out (if applicable in future):** "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link on homepage and in-app settings

### 5. Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.121)

**You have the right to limit our use and disclosure of sensitive personal information** to purposes necessary to perform services or provide goods reasonably expected by consumers.

**Sensitive Personal Information We Collect:**
- Financial account information (via Plaid, with explicit consent)
- Precise geolocation (only if device location enabled)
- Contents of communications (in-app messaging with agents)

**Current Use:** We use sensitive personal information only for disclosed, expected purposes (home buying services). No secondary uses requiring opt-out.

**How to Opt Out (if applicable):** privacy@silverkey.com or in-app settings

### 6. Right to Non-Discrimination (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.125)

**You have the right to not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising your CCPA/CPRA rights.**

We will **NOT**:
- Deny goods or services
- Charge different prices or rates
- Provide different quality of goods or services
- Suggest you will receive different prices or quality

**Financial Incentives:** We may offer financial incentives (e.g., discounts, rewards) for providing certain information, but participation is optional and you may withdraw at any time.

## Verification Procedures

To protect your privacy and security, we verify your identity before fulfilling requests.

### Identity Verification

#### For "Right to Know" Requests (Categories)

**Low-Risk Request:**
- Match name and email to account
- Confirm via email verification link

#### For "Right to Know" Requests (Specific Pieces)

**High-Risk Request (more sensitive data):**
- Match name, email, and one additional data point (e.g., phone number, account creation date)
- Two-factor authentication if enabled
- Signed declaration under penalty of perjury (if necessary)

#### For "Right to Delete" Requests

**High-Risk Request:**
- Same verification as "specific pieces" request
- Additional confirmation step (to prevent unauthorized deletion)
- Confirmation email with 7-day cancellation window

#### For "Right to Correct" Requests

**Medium-Risk Request:**
- Match name and email to account
- Email verification link
- Review of changes before applying

### Account Holders vs. Non-Account Holders

- **Account Holders:** Verification via login credentials + email confirmation
- **Non-Account Holders:** Manual verification (name, email, and additional data point we collected)

### Authorized Agents

- **Additional Verification:** Written authorization + proof of identity for both consumer and agent

### Verification Failure

If we cannot verify your identity, we will inform you and may request additional information. If verification ultimately fails, we will not fulfill the request to protect privacy.
